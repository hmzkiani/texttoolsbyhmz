About TextTools

TextTools is a free collection of simple online utilities designed to make everyday text work faster and easier.

Whether you are a student finishing an essay, a writer polishing a draft, a blogger preparing a post, a developer cleaning up data, a researcher analyzing content, or just someone who needs to count words — TextTools has a small, focused tool for the job.

Who is it for?

  • Students working to a word or character limit
  • Writers and editors tightening drafts
  • Bloggers preparing posts and slugs
  • Developers cleaning data or generating test text
  • Researchers analysing text content
  • Anyone who needs a quick text utility

Our Purpose

Most text tasks require either a bulky desktop app or a website cluttered with ads and sign-up walls. TextTools offers a clean alternative: small tools that each do one thing well, load instantly, and respect your attention.

All 14 core tools run directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is processed on your own device and is not sent to any server for these tools.

What We Offer

  • Counting — words, characters, lines and word frequency.
  • Formatting — case conversion, cleaning, small text, superscript and subscript.
  • Utilities — duplicate removal, find & replace, and slug generation.
  • Generators — Lorem Ipsum and random text.

We keep the site lightweight and fast, and add tools only when they meaningfully help with real text work.
