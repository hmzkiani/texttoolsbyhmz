Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Your Text

The 14 core tools on TextTools process your input entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Your text is not transmitted to our servers or any third party during normal use of these tools.

No Accounts

We do not require registration, and we do not collect personal information to use the tools.

Contact Form

Our contact form uses a mailto link that opens your own email client. We do not receive or store form submissions on a server.

Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). No other data is stored.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts.
